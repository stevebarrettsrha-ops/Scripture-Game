Song of Songs · Kethuḇim — The Writings
Many waters are unable to quench love, nor do floods wash it away. — SHIR HASHIRIM 8:7
The song of songs, which is Shelomoh’s — the beloved and her love through the vineyards and the gardens of spices: “I am my beloved’s, and my beloved is mine,” and love as strong as death, a flame of Yah that many waters cannot quench.
