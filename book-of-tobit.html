Tobit — Ethiopic & Eastern Apocrypha
“I am Raphaʾĕl, one of the seven set-apart messengers who present the prayers of the qodeshim, and enter before the esteem of the Set-apart One.” — TOḆIYAH 12:15

The Book of Toḇiyah

Four acts · TOḆIYAH — the tale of Tobit of Nineveh, who buried the dead of his people by night and was struck blind; and of Sarah of Ecbatana, whose seven husbands the demon Asmodeus had slain — two prayers heard as one in the presence of the great Aluahim. Then the messenger Raphaʾĕl, unknown, guides young Tobias to Media: the great fish of the Tigris and its heart, liver, and gall; the wedding under the shadow of seven graves and the smoke that drove the demon to Mitsrayim bound; the gall that scaled the blindness from Tobit’s eyes; and the guide revealed — “I am Raphaʾĕl” — ascending in light to Him who sent him.
