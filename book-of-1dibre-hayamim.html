The Book of 1 Diḇrĕ HaYamim

Four acts · 1 DIḆRĔ HAYAMIM — the Chronicles of Dawiḏ: from the line kept from Aḏam to the son of Yahshai, the fall of Sha’ul on Gilboa and the anointing at Ḥeḇron; through the mighty men — the water of the well of Bĕyth Leḥem poured out to YAHUAH, and Benayah down in the pit with the lion on a snowy day; through the ark brought up — the wrath at the threshing-floor and Uzza, then borne on the shoulders of the Lĕwites with dancing, the song of thanks at the Tent, and the berith of an everlasting house; to the threshing-floor of Ornan where fire fell from the shamayim, the charge to Shelomoh to be strong and build, and the great qahal: “Yours, O YAHUAH, is the greatness — Yours is the reign”
