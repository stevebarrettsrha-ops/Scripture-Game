Ecclesiastes · Kethuḇim — The Writings
The end of the matter — all having been heard: Fear Aluahim and guard His commands, for this applies to all mankind. — QOHELETH 12:13
The words of the Preacher, son of Dawiḏ, sovereign in Yerushalayim — “Vanity of vanities, all is vanity”: the sun and the wind and the rivers that never fill the sea, a time for every matter under the shamayim, and the whole conclusion — fear Aluahim, and guard His commands.
