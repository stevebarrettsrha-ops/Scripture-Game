The Besorah of Yaḥuchanon

Nine acts · twenty-one chapters · YAHUCHANON — the witness that YAHUSHA is the Word made flesh, the Son of Aluahim, that believing you might have life in His Name: from the Word in the beginning and the witness of Yoḥanan the Immerser and the first taught ones; through the seven signs — the water turned to wine at Qanah, the official’s son, the man at the pool of Bĕyth Ḥesda, the bread of the five thousand, the walking on the sea, the man born blind, and the raising of Elʿazar; the “I AM” of the Word — the bread of life, the light of the world, the door and the Good Shepherd, the resurrection and the life, the way and the truth and the life, the true vine; Niqodemon by night and the woman at the well, the woman they would stone, and the washing of feet and the new command; the farewell and the Helper and the prayer of the Hour; the garden, Ḥanan and Qayapha, Pilate and “Behold the Man,” the stake and “It is accomplished”; the empty tomb and Miryam, Toma and the wounds, and the breakfast by the Sea of Tiḇeryah — “Do you love Me? Feed My sheep.”
