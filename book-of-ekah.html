Through the kindnesses of YAHUAH we are not consumed, for His compassions never fail; they are new every morning. — ĔḴAH 3:22–23
Five laments over the fallen city — “How lonely sits the city that was full of people!” — the ways of Tsiyon mourning, the ash and the sword; yet in the midst of the ruins, the great turning: “His compassions never fail; they are new every morning; great is Your trustworthiness.”
