BARUḴ
Baruch · Ethiopic & Eastern Apocrypha
Hear the mitsvot of life, O Yasharal; give ear, and learn wisdom. — BARUḴ 3:9
The words of Baruch son of Neriyah, written in Baḇel after Yahrushalayim was burned — the confession of a people in exile and a prayer for mercy, then a hymn to wisdom: the mitsvot of life that Yasharal forsook. Yet the mourning turns to comfort — “Take off the garment of your sorrow, O Yahrushalayim” — and the promise of the joy that comes from Aluahim, who leads Yasharal home in the light of His esteem.
